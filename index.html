Concept A — Turn Digest: every round becomes one card, one line per actor, numbers bolded and color-coded. Older rounds fade and condense automatically so the newest round is always the most readable thing on screen.

Concept B — Highlight + Ticker: the single biggest thing that just happened gets a large banner, impossible to miss even glancing quickly. Everything else sits in a smaller, denser ticker below for anyone who wants the full detail.

Both are taller and live higher on the screen than the current log, and both cut the number of things you'd have to read per round roughly in half by grouping rather than listing every individual message.
