What's different from the current journal: the art becomes the actual backdrop the dialogue sits on top of, rather than a separate block above plain text — the fade gradient is what makes the text readable regardless of what's in the image. One line at a time, tap to advance, portrait circle keeps the speaker identity clear. Narrator lines drop the portrait and mute the styling, so it reads as scene-setting rather than a character talking.

Worth deciding: should every chapter show art the whole time (like here), or only during the opening line, with the art fading out once dialogue starts? Full-time art looks better but only works well for chapters that already have generated art — most don't yet.
